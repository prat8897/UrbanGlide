Case Study

BookMyShow

A mission-critical logistics execution across India.

One deadline. Multiple challenges. Zero missed windows.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    BookMyShow reached out for urgent event logistics across multiple cities.

    Fixed event dates. Zero room for delay.

  2. 02

    Understanding

    We captured every detail — locations, timelines, materials, access, dependencies and potential risks.

  3. 03

    Planning

    Routes, vehicles and partners planned for each sector.

    Backups and contingencies built in.

  4. 04

    Challenges

    No trucks on critical points. Airport coordination delays.

    Emergency additional shipments. Difficult terrain in Shillong.

  5. 05

    Our Decisions

    Used cabs where trucks weren't available. Coordinated directly with airport cargo terminals.

    Activated backup partners instantly.

  6. 06

    We Paid to Deliver

    We paid out of our own pocket for cab movements and extra costs — because the event deadline mattered more.

  7. 07

    Outcome

    All shipments delivered on time.

    Emergency requests handled successfully. Scope expanded.

  8. 08

    Renewal

    The client trusted us with the next contract.

    A partnership that continues today.

The result: a partnership built on reliability, not just transactions.
